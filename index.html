03 — How a website with me goes

Four steps, no mystery.

  1. 01

    Hello — 20 minutes

    A call or a long email. What you're selling, who it's for, three sites you like. I tell you honestly if I'm the wrong person.

  2. 02

    Sketch — days

    You get a homepage draft in a few days, in both languages if needed. We argue about it early, while changes are cheap.

  3. 03

    Build — 1–3 weeks

    Hand-coded, no page builder. You get a live link and watch it grow. Two revision rounds included.

  4. 04

    Launch + handover

    Deployed, measured, documented. You own the code and the domain. I stay a message away for a month, free.
